The problem underneath the business

Indian policy talks about C2+50% — a price covering a farmer's full cost plus a fifty percent margin. Most small farmers never see close to it. Without storage, and needing cash immediately, they sell the morning the crop comes off the field at whatever that day's price is: frequently 10–30% below MSP, sometimes below what it cost to grow.

Sourcing directly and removing layers is the only lever that moves this. It's why the farmer app exists, and it's the argument for the business itself.
