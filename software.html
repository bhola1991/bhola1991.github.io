Live on Google Play

Job Triage

2026 · mine end to end

Reads any CV, works out which career directions are realistically open to that person, then finds and ranks jobs. Every role gets two separate scores: how well the work fits, and whether that employer will actually reply. A perfect job you won't get is not a good lead.

Reads Greenhouse, Lever and Ashby feeds directly rather than scraping, with confidence tracked separately from score.
